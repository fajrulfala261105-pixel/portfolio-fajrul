PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the Project

This project presents an e-commerce sales and customer analytics dashboard designed to summarize business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs) and visual analysis.

The dashboard provides an overview of total revenue, total orders, total customers, and average order value. It also explores monthly revenue trends, top-performing products, revenue by country, and customer distribution across different segments.

Dashboard Components

Monthly Revenue Trend

Examines monthly revenue fluctuations to identify changes in sales performance throughout the displayed period.

Top Products by Revenue

Compares product revenue to highlight products contributing to the sales total.

Top Countries by Revenue

Shows revenue distribution across countries, including the United Kingdom, Netherlands, EIRE, and Germany.

Customer Segmentation

Compares customer counts across segments, including Lost / Low Value, Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, and Potential Loyalists.

OBSERVATIONS

Key Dashboard Observations

01

Revenue Fluctuations

Monthly revenue varies across the displayed period, with the highest labeled value appearing in November 2011 at 1,503,867.

02

Country Revenue Concentration

The United Kingdom contributes the largest revenue shown in the country chart, at 9,001,744.

03

Customer Segment Distribution

Lost / Low Value is the largest displayed customer segment with 1,568 customers, followed by Champions with 933 customers.

These observations describe the values displayed in the dashboard. They do not, by themselves, establish the causes of revenue changes or customer behavior.

CONCLUSION

Project Summary

This dashboard brings together sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, geographic revenue distribution, and customer segmentation in a single visual interface.

By presenting key indicators alongside charts, the dashboard supports an overview of e-commerce business performance and helps identify areas that may require further analysis.
